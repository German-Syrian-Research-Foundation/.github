# Code of Conduct

*Deutsch-Syrische Forschungsstiftung e.V. (DSFG)*

## Our commitment

DSFG exists to open academic doors for people who have often had them closed. That mission only works if our spaces — repositories, issues, pull requests, discussions, and the events they support — are safe and welcoming for everyone.

We pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for all, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, immigration or residence status, personal appearance, race, caste, colour, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our values in practice

**We expect participants to:**

- Assume good faith and give constructive, specific feedback
- Be patient with contributors working in a second, third, or fourth language — English is not the first language of most of our community
- Respect that many contributors volunteer around studies, jobs, and family, in different time zones
- Accept responsibility, apologise to those affected by our mistakes, and learn from them
- Prioritise what is best for the community and the students and researchers we serve

**Unacceptable behaviour includes:**

- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention or advances
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information — email addresses, phone numbers, addresses, immigration or asylum status, or physical location — without their explicit permission
- Sectarian, ethnic, national, or religious hostility of any kind
- Using DSFG spaces to campaign for political parties, movements, or armed groups
- Any other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional or academic setting

### A note on political and personal safety

Many members of our community have family, colleagues, or professional exposure in Syria and the wider region. Political disagreement is a normal part of life, but **DSFG spaces are not the place for it.** Content that could expose an individual to risk from any authority or armed actor — naming people, locations, affiliations, or travel — will be removed immediately and without debate.

## Scope

This Code applies within all DSFG GitHub repositories and community spaces, and when an individual is officially representing DSFG in public — for example using an `@ds-fg.com` address, posting from an official account, or acting as an appointed representative at an online or offline event.

## Reporting

Report abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behaviour to:

📧 **[info@ds-fg.com](mailto:info@ds-fg.com)**

Please do **not** send conduct reports to `github@ds-fg.com` — that address is a technical inbox for security and bug reports, and is read by a wider group.

All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly. We will respect the privacy and security of the reporter. If your report concerns a board member or maintainer, you may address it to any individual board member directly; that person will recuse the subject of the report from handling it.

Reports may be made anonymously, though this can limit what we are able to investigate.

## Enforcement

Community leaders will follow these guidelines in determining consequences:

| Level | Behaviour | Consequence |
|:--|:--|:--|
| **1. Correction** | Inappropriate language or unprofessional conduct | Private written warning explaining the violation; a public apology may be requested |
| **2. Warning** | A single incident or a series of actions | Formal warning with conditions; no interaction with those involved for a specified period. Violating these terms escalates to a ban |
| **3. Temporary ban** | Serious violation or sustained inappropriate behaviour | Temporary ban from all community interaction and public communication with DSFG |
| **4. Permanent ban** | Pattern of violation, harassment, or aggression toward individuals or groups | Permanent ban from all public community interaction, and referral to the DSFG Board where membership status is affected |
