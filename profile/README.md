## Who we are

The **German-Syrian Research Foundation** (*Deutsch-Syrische Forschungsstiftung e.V.*, DSFG) is a non-profit, non-governmental organisation founded in Germany in 2016 by Syrians and Germans of Syrian origin. We are registered at the Amtsgericht Frankfurt am Main (VR 16109) and recognised as a charitable organisation (*gemeinnützig*).

We support Syrian and Arab students and researchers in their higher studies and careers, and help them access scholarships and academic opportunities in Germany.

> **"Active Minds, Productive Ideas"**

## Our programmes

| Programme | Focus |
|:--|:--|
| [Academic Empowerment Scholarship](https://ds-fg.com/service/aes/) | Scholarships for Syrian and Arab students in Germany |
| [Academic Mentoring (Maabar)](https://ds-fg.com/service/mentoring/) | One-to-one academic and social mentoring by volunteers |
| [DSFG Data Camp](https://ds-fg.com/service/dsfg-datacamp-program/) | Digital and data skills training for students and researchers |

| [ASSG](https://arabscissym.com) · [SAFIERR](https://safierr.com) | Our annual scientific symposium and academic reform forum |

## A note on data protection

DSFG operates under German and EU law. **No personal data** — applicant records, member data, scholarship applications, health or immigration information — may be stored in this GitHub organisation, in public *or* private repositories. Personal data stays in our approved, contracted systems. See the [contributing guide](https://github.com/German-Syrian-Research-Foundation/.github/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md#data-protection) for details.
